# 🌌 Celestial Casino (DevHacks 2026)
*Also known as 21JumpStreet-Hackathon2026!*

## 🎲 Welcome to the Casino!
Come join us and play Blackjack and GAMBLE!!!!

**Celestial Casino** is a 3D Unity-based game developed for **DevHacks 2026**. We brought the thrill of the casino to life with a fully functional Blackjack experience, complete with a cinematic Star Wars-style intro and themed sound effects. (Slots coming soon 👀).

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
* **Game Engine:** Unity 3D
* **Programming Language:** C#
* **3D Assets & Environment:** Blender

## ✨ Features
* **Playable 3D Blackjack:** A complete game loop featuring card dealing, dynamic betting mechanics, Ace evaluation, Hit, Stand, and Double Down logic.
* **Custom Environment:** A fully modeled and textured custom 3D Blackjack table.
* **Cinematic Experience:** A custom Star Wars-style intro sequence and themed sound effects that trigger during in-game events.
